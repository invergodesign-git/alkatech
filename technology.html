Start with the 8.5 pH (1st level) for one to two weeks, then move to 9.0 pH. Most adults settle on 9.5 pH. Do not drink the Turbo level (11.0 pH) or acidic water; use those for cooking and cleaning. Use purified (filtered only) water when taking medication. If you have health conditions, consult your doctor first.

The display counts down from 4,000 liters on filter #1 and 8,000 liters on filter #2, and tells you when to change them at 70 liters remaining. With average use, replace filters about once a year, or sooner if the water smells bad or the flow drops.

It does, just more intelligently. Instead of running on a timer, the ionizer senses contamination on the plates and runs a cleaning cycle only when needed, so you may not see one for a couple of months. We also recommend cleaning the plates with an internal cleaning filter at least once a year.

It’s best to drink it fresh. Stored in an airtight glass or polycarbonate bottle (never plastic) in the refrigerator, it keeps its alkalinity for about three days. In a container that isn’t airtight, it returns to a neutral pH in about twelve hours.
